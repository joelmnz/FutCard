# FutCard roster — 2026/27 season snapshot (verified 2026-09-29)

Dated, curated real-player card templates for newly generated cards. Club, nation and position for every outfield player below were verified against the linked Transfermarkt squad pages (season 2026/27, squad as served on 2026-09-29); dual-nation players carry the national team recorded on their Transfermarkt profile. Players whose affiliation could not be verified were omitted. The original outfield snapshot is retained; goalkeeper support was added using official club profiles fetched 2026-10-02 (see the goalkeeper addendum).

## Sources

- **Primary (per-player club/position/nation/age):** Transfermarkt club detailed-squad pages, `…/kader/verein/{id}/saison_id/2026/plus/1`, fetched 2026-09-29 (one URL per club below).
- **Nationality of dual-flag players:** Transfermarkt player profiles (`…/profil/spieler/{id}`, fetched 2026-09-29), `Current international`/`National player` field; profile URL is listed on those players' rows.
- **League membership cross-check:** Wikipedia season articles for each league, fetched 2026-09-29 (e.g. [2026–27 Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026%E2%80%9327_Premier_League)), plus the Transfermarkt league pages (`…/startseite/wettbewerb/{GB1,ES1,IT1,L1,FR1}/saison_id/2026`).
- **Stats:** authored plausible profiles (position-archetype shapes with hand-tuned values for star players). They are gameplay values, not verified performance data.

The outfield data is a fixed snapshot of club affiliation on 2026-09-29, not a claim about later transfers; goalkeeper affiliations are separately dated 2026-10-02. The snapshot is intentionally frozen; refreshing it is a future decision.

## Derivation contract (`src/app.js`)

- `overall` = position-weighted mean of the five stats (`OVR_WEIGHTS`), rounded.
- `rarity`: overall ≥ 85 legendary · 75–84 epic · 65–74 rare · else common.
- `basePrice`: piecewise-linear monotonic by overall through the legacy rarity price bands (common 50–64 → 1,000–15,000 · rare 65–74 → 15,000–80,000 · epic 75–84 → 80,000–500,000 · legendary 85–99 → 500,000–2,000,000). Stronger players always rate and price ≥ weaker ones.
